About Mechanical Engineering

Mechanical Engineering deals with the design, analysis, manufacturing and maintenance of mechanical systems. It brings together concepts from mechanics, materials, thermal science and production technology.

Students can build their foundation by studying engineering drawings, workshop processes, machine elements, material properties and the fundamentals of thermodynamics.

Practical work helps connect theoretical knowledge with manufacturing, maintenance and industrial applications.
