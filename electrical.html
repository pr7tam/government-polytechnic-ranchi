Discover the Department

Understanding the world of electrical systems.

Electrical Engineering covers the generation, transmission, distribution and utilisation of electrical energy, along with electrical machines, circuits and measurement systems.

About Electrical Engineering

Electrical Engineering focuses on the principles and applications of electricity, electrical equipment, power systems and electrical measurements.

Students can strengthen their understanding through circuit analysis, electrical machine studies, laboratory experiments, safety practices and technical projects.

The aim is to connect theoretical concepts with practical engineering applications and responsible use of electrical systems.

Learn by Doing

From circuit theory to practical understanding.

Laboratory learning helps students understand circuit behaviour, practise measurements, analyse results and develop safe engineering habits under appropriate supervision.

Electrical equipment should only be handled with proper training, supervision and safety precautions.
