Connecting electronic systems and communication.

Electronics and Communication Engineering brings together electronic devices, circuits, digital systems, signals and communication technologies.

About Electronics & Communication

This field focuses on the principles behind electronic components, circuit behaviour, digital logic, signals and systems used to communicate information.

Students can develop their foundations through circuit analysis, electronic measurements, digital electronics, communication principles and introductory embedded systems.

Practical experiments and technical projects help connect theory with real-world electronic and communication applications.

Learn by Doing

From electronic theory to practical experiments.

Practical learning can help students understand circuit behaviour, measure electronic quantities, examine digital systems and build small technology projects.

Electronic equipment should be handled carefully, with appropriate supervision and electrical safety practices.
