Discover the Department

Designing the foundations of modern infrastructure.

Civil Engineering deals with the planning, design, construction and maintenance of infrastructure such as buildings, roads, bridges and water systems.

About Civil Engineering

Civil Engineering applies scientific and engineering principles to the built environment, including structures, transportation, construction and water-related systems.

Students can develop their foundations through engineering drawing, surveying, construction materials, building technology and introductory structural concepts.

Practical activities help connect classroom knowledge with measurements, site observations, drawings and construction processes.

Learn by Doing

From engineering drawings to real-world structures.

Practical learning can help students understand site measurements, material properties, drawing interpretation, construction sequences and quality observations.

Construction sites require suitable protective equipment, proper supervision and compliance with safety procedures.
